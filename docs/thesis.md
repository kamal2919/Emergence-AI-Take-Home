# Investment thesis and scoring rubric

## Thesis

SignalDesk is screening seed-stage companies that automate a recurring,
operational workflow for SMB or mid-market customers. The first product must be
specific enough to evaluate in a sales call: a buyer, a workflow, an integration
surface, and a measurable before/after outcome.

The attractive wedge is not "AI agents." It is a workflow where software can
replace expensive or slow human coordination, the customer can verify the work,
and repeated use can create workflow data or integration lock-in.

## Scoring (100 points)

| Dimension | Points | What earns the points |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Workflow pain and buyer clarity | 25 | A named operational buyer and frequent, costly job |
| Wedge and implementation path | 20 | Narrow first use case; realistic integrations / deployment |
| Evidence of demand | 20 | Specific launch, customer, usage, or technical signal |
| Team signal | 15 | Relevant domain, startup, or technical evidence; unknown is not a penalty disguised as a fact |
| Defensibility | 10 | Workflow data, distribution, integration, or domain advantage |
| Execution / risk adjustment | 10 | Low regulatory, reliability, and go-to-market concentration risk |

## Recommendation bands

- **Take a meeting (75-100):** evidence and the initial wedge are sufficiently
  concrete to spend partner time validating the key risks.
- **Watch (55-74):** plausible company, but an important proof point is missing.
- **Pass (0-54):** does not fit the thesis today, or the evidence is inadequate.

Scores are triage tools, not valuations. A high score is a reason to learn more,
not a claim that the investment should be made.
